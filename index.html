A king in retirement. A puppy in charge.

Crown & Chaos is a short-form dog comedy show. Taurus, a posh senior shih tzu, tries to keep the peace. Muiji, a one-year-old maltipoo, has other plans.

Watch the show

Meet the cast

Taurus

Retired king, senior shih tzu

Posh, dry and endlessly dignified. He has given up the throne, not the opinions.

Muiji

Spunky 1-year-old maltipoo

Fast, loud and fearless. Every quiet moment is an invitation to cause chaos.

The show

Short comedy episodes about two dogs sharing one kingdom. New episodes are posted every:

  • Tuesday
  • Thursday
  • Sunday

Between episodes we post standalone shorts: quick gags, reactions and everyday moments.

About this site and our posting tool

This is the home of the Crown & Chaos creators. We use our own scheduling tool, Crown and Chaos Poster, to publish our own videos to our own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. It is private, used only by us, and it never posts for anyone else. This site has no ads, no trackers and no scripts.
