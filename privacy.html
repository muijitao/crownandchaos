Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Crown and Chaos Poster connects only to accounts owned by the Crown & Chaos creator.

What we collect: from TikTok, the basic profile (display name, avatar, username), account stats and the list of our own videos, plus access tokens needed to upload and publish.

How we use it: only to publish our videos and show their status. We do not sell, share or use the data for advertising.

Storage: on our own computer, in a self-hosted Postiz install. Nothing is stored for any other person.

Deleting: revoke access in TikTok settings (Security > Manage app permissions) or message us on Instagram (@crownandchaos.dogs) and we delete the stored tokens.
